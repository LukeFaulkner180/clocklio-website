Terms for business customers using the DayRidge software.
Version 2026-10-30 · Updated 30 October 2026.

Who these terms apply to

These terms govern business-to-business access to DayRidge, supplied by Luke Faulkner trading as DayRidge (“DayRidge”, “we”, “us”). The customer is the business named in its order confirmation (“Customer”). The person accepting confirms they are authorised to bind that business. The service is not a personal consumer product. Your individual quote or written order confirmation sets the package, employee allowance, pricing, start date and any negotiated changes; those specific written terms take precedence over these general terms.

The service

DayRidge supplies hosted workforce software for staff scheduling, time and attendance, employee records and optional functions specified in the Customer’s plan. Features and add-ons are limited to those actually enabled for the Customer. Access is a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence for the agreed business and its authorised users during the agreed term. DayRidge may improve functionality and security without materially removing paid core functionality during the paid term, unless reasonably necessary to comply with law or protect users.

Trial access

Free or discounted trial access applies only for the dates, features and users confirmed in writing. A trial does not automatically become a paid subscription and no payment is due unless the Customer separately accepts a paid order. Unless extended or converted, trial access may end at the trial expiry date. Customer data remains subject to the Data Processing Agreement during a trial when personal data is used. Customers should avoid unnecessary sensitive data while evaluating the service.

Prices and payment

The agreed order specifies the annual subscription fee, whether any VAT is chargeable, due dates and the payment method. Where DayRidge agrees instalments, these spread the cost of a 12-month subscription and do not convert the agreement into a month-to-month contract. Written quotations may offer annual, ten-instalment standing-order or two-instalment arrangements. No payment-card collection or automatic charge is implied by the website. Invoices or written payment instructions will be supplied separately. If payment is overdue, we will notify the Customer and allow a reasonable opportunity to remedy the breach before suspending access, except in serious circumstances.

Add-ons and promotional periods

Optional features may carry separate fees and limits described in the order. Where DayRidge includes add-ons free for an introductory period (for example, three months), they are free for that agreed period and will not become chargeable without an express subsequent quotation and Customer agreement. When the free period ends, the relevant add-on may be disabled unless further access is agreed. Any data preservation/export arrangements should be discussed before disabling an add-on.

Term, renewal and cancellation

The subscription begins and ends on the dates in the Customer’s written order, ordinarily covering 12 months. Renewal is not automatic unless expressly agreed in writing. DayRidge will provide a new quote or renewal terms before a further paid period. The Customer may tell us it does not wish to renew. Either party may terminate for a material breach not remedied within a reasonable period after notice, or where continued performance would be unlawful. Refunds or adjustments are dealt with under applicable law and the agreed order; this clause does not exclude any remedy required by law.

Customer duties

The Customer controls which staff it invites, the permissions granted, the accuracy of employment records and its own payroll, working-time and employment-law compliance. The Customer must use the service lawfully, protect credentials, maintain an independent record or export process appropriate to its business, tell DayRidge promptly of suspected account compromise, and ensure it provides employees any required privacy information. Customers must not attempt unauthorised access to another business or interfere with the service.

Data protection and confidentiality

Where DayRidge processes employee or other Customer personal data on the Customer’s behalf, the Data Processing Agreement forms part of this contract. The Customer is normally the data controller and DayRidge the processor for workspace data. Both parties will protect confidential non-public information, subject to disclosure required by law. Our separate Application Privacy Notice explains data we process for our own purposes.

Availability, support and changes

DayRidge aims to provide a dependable service but does not promise uninterrupted availability, guaranteed disaster-recovery times or a particular support response time unless these are separately agreed in writing. We may undertake maintenance and respond to security incidents. Report issues using the Contact DayRidge page. Time and pay figures are estimates and record-keeping aids, not professional payroll, tax, legal or employment advice.

Any limitation of liability, indemnity or service-credit arrangement expressly agreed in an individual order prevails. These terms do not limit either party’s statutory data-protection duties.

Suspension and ending access

We may suspend accounts as reasonably necessary for security, unlawful use or unresolved serious contract breaches, giving advance notice where practicable. At the end of service, the Customer may request data return or deletion in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement. The Customer should retrieve any available exports before access ends. Contract provisions which by their nature continue (including confidentiality and accrued payment obligations) survive termination.

Changes to material contractual terms will be communicated; a new version requiring acceptance will be presented to the authorised business owner. If part of these terms is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue. For questions or a copy of the agreed order, Contact DayRidge.
