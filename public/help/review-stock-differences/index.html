STOCK MANAGEMENT

Review till differences and investigation notes

Compare the same reporting period, explain discrepancies and follow investigations through.

Full Workspace + Stock add-on4 min read
BEFORE YOU START

Use matching dates and product units for the stock count and till totals. Check deliveries, waste and selling prices before interpreting a difference.

  1. 01

    Open the relevant stocktake

    Review the quantities and movements recorded for the period. A missing delivery or incorrect count can change the expected sales figure.

  2. 02

    Enter actual till takings

    Use the matching period and product total from the till. Version 0.1 requires this figure to be entered manually.

  3. 03

    Compare expected and actual

    The reconciliation shows expected takings, actual till takings and the difference. A shortfall needs investigation; a difference alone does not establish its cause.

  4. 04

    Add an investigation note

    Record a useful explanation such as spillage, a counting correction or a promotion. Keep the note open while checks are outstanding.

  5. 05

    Resolve and review trends

    Mark the investigation resolved when the checks are complete. Use product and reporting-period analytics to follow stock usage, wastage and recurring differences.

A useful detail

Till reconciliation is a checking tool. Review the underlying counts and business records before drawing conclusions about a discrepancy.

WHO THIS GUIDE IS FOR

Business Owner or authorised stock Manager
