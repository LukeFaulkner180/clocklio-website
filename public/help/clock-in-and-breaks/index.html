ROTAS & TIME

Clock in, record breaks and clock out

Use your employee login or the registered workplace kiosk to record the working day.

Clockx + Full Workspace3 min read
BEFORE YOU START

Use the login invited by your business. Workplace kiosk access uses your employee number and PIN. A Manager who works shifts needs a linked employee profile.

  1. 01

    Open your time clock

    From your employee view, open My Time Clock. Alternatively, use the business’s registered workplace kiosk.

  2. 02

    Clock in at the start

    Check your current clock status and record the start of work. A rota entry alone does not clock you in.

  3. 03

    Record breaks

    Use the available break controls during the shift. Break records affect the recorded hours shown in your timesheet.

  4. 04

    Clock out when finished

    Finish the shift through the time clock. Check My hours for the resulting completed record.

  5. 05

    Report missing or incorrect entries

    Ask an authorised Manager to review a missing shift or incorrect times. Managers can correct completed timesheets; the activity history records changes.

A useful detail

Pay figures are gross estimates based on recorded time and saved rates. Your employer’s payroll process handles deductions, statutory calculations and payslips.

WHO THIS GUIDE IS FOR

Employee or working Manager
