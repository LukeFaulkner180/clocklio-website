PEOPLE & DOCUMENTS

Share contracts and company documents

Prepare a private employee contract or upload guidance for the business team.

Clockx + Full Workspace4 min read
BEFORE YOU START

Prepare the document you intend to share and decide whether it belongs to one employee or the whole company. The contract outline is editable and should reflect the terms you have agreed.

  1. 01

    Open Documents

    Managers can create a contract or upload a company document. An optional contract can also be prepared when creating an employee.

  2. 02

    Choose the right audience

    An employee contract belongs to the selected employee. Company policies, procedures and handbooks belong in the company-document section.

  3. 03

    Review before sharing

    Check the title, employee, contents and acknowledgement requirement. Keep unfinished material as a draft until it is ready.

  4. 04

    Publish the document

    Share it when your checks are complete. Employees see their own shared contracts and the company guidance available to their business.

  5. 05

    Follow acknowledgements

    Use document receipts to see who has acknowledged a published document. A read acknowledgement records receipt; it is not an electronic contract-signing service.

A useful detail

Private employee documents are limited to their creator, the Business Owner, authorised file Managers and the relevant employee where shared. Payroll and wider personnel records have separate access controls.

WHO THIS GUIDE IS FOR

Managers publish; employees read their documents
