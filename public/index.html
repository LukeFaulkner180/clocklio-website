BEFORE YOU START

A few useful details about your workspace.

What is included in the annual plan?

The full workspace brings together rota planning, time and attendance, people tools and documents. It includes five employees for £299.99 a year. Employees six to nine add £45 each per year; from ten, fixed annual plans apply. Clockx starts at £99.99 a year with rotas, clocking, annual leave, extra hours, payroll hours, contracts and business documents. Fixed tiers cover five, ten or twenty employees. Tasks and Stock Management are optional monthly add-ons. Compare the plans.

Is it only for hospitality?

The workspace is designed for cafés, shops, hospitality venues and other businesses with teams to organise. Stock Management supports units such as bottles, measures and individual items, so businesses can use the units that fit their products.

Can I control what each person can access?

Yes. Owners, Managers, Employees and Team Leaders have views suited to their roles. Business owners can control additional Manager and Team Leader permissions, including access to specific tools and records. Explore the features.
