DAYRIDGE · TRAINING GUIDE

Manage clocking, kiosks and breaks

Set up the shared kiosk, review active staff and correct worked time without deducting a break twice.

Business owners · ManagersClockx + Full Workspace
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role.

Set up and use the kiosk

The kiosk is the shared workplace clocking view. It uses employee numbers and personal PINs, with manager access protected by a separate kiosk PIN.

Where to go Time clock / Rota > On-site kiosk

  1. Choose the workplace device. Open the correct business workspace and enter the workplace kiosk through the supported manager control.
  2. Complete device setup if requested. Use the manager setup flow for the workplace device. Confirm the business shown is the one whose staff will use it.
  3. Keep manager access private. The manager kiosk PIN is six digits. Store and communicate it only to the people who should unlock or exit the protected kiosk.
  4. Explain employee clocking. Employees enter their own employee number and personal PIN, then follow the clock-in or clock-out flow shown on the device.
  5. Check a real result. After a pilot clocking, confirm the correct employee appears in the team clock status. At the end, confirm the completed record and break information.
  • Do not use one employee number for several staff.
  • Do not leave the owner’s private dashboard open on the shared kiosk.
  • If a clocking fails, check the number, PIN and device state before creating another employee.
Kiosk and personal clocking are different routes

Employees may also have My Time Clock in their signed-in account. Explain which route your business expects them to use.

Clock in, clock out and check

Published shifts describe the plan. Successful clock-ins and clock-outs create the worked-time record used in attendance and payroll-hour summaries.

Where to go My Time Clock / Manager > Time clock

  1. Employee starts work. Use the personal clocking page or workplace kiosk. Wait for the app to confirm the clock-in; an unsaved tap is not a recorded shift.
  2. Employee checks the active shift. My Time Clock shows whether they are clocked in, the elapsed timer and a current earnings estimate where available.
  3. Manager checks the team. Use the team clock status to review who is working. The manager’s own personal shift should use their linked employee profile.
  4. Employee finishes work. Choose Clock out and complete the confirmation. Check the result rather than leaving the page during saving.
  5. Resolve a missed clocking. An authorised Manager can clock an employee in or out and adjust the relevant time through supported manager controls. Record the actual agreed time, not a convenient default.
A connection problem after clock-in

A clock-in already saved to DayRidge is not erased because the page closes or briefly becomes unavailable. When access returns, refresh and check the active record. A failed or unconfirmed new action still needs review.

The elapsed timer and earnings display are live estimates. Unpaid breaks and authorised corrections can change the final worked hours.

Break rules and time corrections

Review recorded breaks and saved automatic rules together. The same break should not be deducted twice.

Where to go Business / Time clock > Past shifts

Worked-hours example

09:00 to 17:00 = 8 elapsed hours. Less a 30-minute unpaid break = 7 hours 30 minutes paid.

  • Configure new shifts: in Business, set whether automatic unpaid breaks are enabled, the shift length that must be exceeded and the unpaid minutes to apply.
  • Understand the saved rule: new clock-ins and manually added shifts use the current rule. Existing shifts keep the rule saved with them; changing today’s setting does not rewrite every old record.
  • Compare manual and automatic breaks: where a longer recorded break exists, that value is used. Review the displayed total unpaid break rather than adding the two deductions together.
  • Correct a completed shift: find the employee’s past shift, use Edit, review start, end and unpaid break, then save. Check overnight dates carefully.
  • Delete only an erroneous entry: read the confirmation and check the employee and dates. A genuine worked shift normally needs a correction rather than deletion.
Check before payroll

The example shows 8 elapsed hours less a 30-minute unpaid break, giving 7.5 paid hours. Your business rule and the actual saved record determine the real result.
