Tasks and shared shift checklists

Assign work, build reusable checklists, let staff take ownership and capture live photo evidence.

Business owners · Managers · EmployeesFull Workspace + Tasks add-on
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role.

Assign individual work

Use individual tasks when a particular employee needs a clear action, instructions and deadline. Use shared shift checklists for a job that only needs doing once.

Where to go Tasks / Employee > My Tasks

  1. Confirm activation. Tasks is a paid add-on for Full Workspace. Ask DayRidge to enable it for the purchasing business and confirm the agreed chargeable-user count.
  2. Create the task. Enter a concise title, useful instructions and a due date where needed. Explain what finished work should look like.
  3. Assign the right people. Select the intended employee or employees. For individual assignments, each person’s progress is tracked separately.
  4. Employee progresses the work. In My Tasks, they review instructions and move their own assignment through To do, In progress and Complete using the available controls.
  5. Review the result. Use the manager view to check completion and overdue work. Follow up on missing work through the business’s normal management process.
Use individual tasks forUse shift checklists for
A named employee’s specific jobA shared opening, closing or shift check
A personal deadline or follow-upOne task claimed by one person from eligible shifts
Separate progress per assignmentStructured answers and optional required live photos
Keep the task list useful

Give tasks a clear owner and sensible due date. Avoid repeating a shared job as multiple individual assignments when the team only needs it completed once.

Build a reusable shift checklist

A checklist template defines the job. Allocating it to published shifts creates a shared task for that working day.

Where to go Tasks > Shift checklists

  1. 01Create template
  2. 02Select shifts
  3. 03One person claims
  4. 04Review completion
  1. Create the template. Give the checklist a title and instructions. Examples include opening checks, a handover or a close-down routine.
  2. Add structured fields. Choose checkboxes, text answers, numbers or selection options. Mark the fields that are required and make question wording specific enough to answer consistently.
  3. Choose evidence requirements. Enable Require photo evidence where the job genuinely benefits from a fresh photograph. Do not require unnecessary images of people or private information.
  4. Allocate to the shift. Choose the saved template, the working date and the relevant published employee shifts. Publish the rota first if no shifts are available.
  5. Check the shared run. The selected shift group shares a checklist that only needs completing once. The board shows Available, In progress or Completed, who took it and the relevant timestamps.
Completion is recorded, not a separate approval stage

Managers can inspect answers, evidence and completion details. Do not describe this as an appraisal, electronic signature or a separate manager sign-off workflow.

Take ownership and finish the job

Employees can claim an available shared shift task, complete the required answers and capture fresh evidence in the app.

Where to go Employee > My Tasks

  1. Choose active work. The active view shows new and in-progress work. Completed tasks are available in their separate view instead of sitting under the main list.
  2. Take the task. Open an available checklist and choose Take task. Check that your name appears as the person who has taken it. Other colleagues can see that it is already owned.
  3. Answer the checklist. Read the instructions and complete required fields. Use the right units for numeric answers and explain relevant exceptions in supported text fields.
  4. Take live evidence where needed. Choose Take photo, allow camera access and capture the scene in the branded camera dialog. Gallery uploads are disabled in this workflow. Required-photo checklists need at least one live image.
  5. Complete or release. Submit completion when the job is finished. If you cannot do it, use the supported release action to return the task to the board rather than leaving it claimed indefinitely.

A checklist can hold up to five photos. If the camera is blocked, review the browser’s camera permission for the DayRidge app and try again. Capture the task itself; a fresh photo does not replace a manager’s review of the actual work.

Manager follow-up

Inspect the recorded answers, claimant and time, and open evidence photos where needed. Use the Completed tasks area to find finished work.
