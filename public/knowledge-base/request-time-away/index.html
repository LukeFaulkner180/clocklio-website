Knowledge base/Leave & sickness
DAYRIDGE · TRAINING GUIDE

Request holiday and report time away

Send a leave request, check its decision and keep sickness separate from holiday.

EmployeesHoliday: both packages. Sickness: Full Workspace.
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role.

Request annual leave

Use your own leave area, then wait for a Manager’s decision.

Where to go My leave (Clockx) / My shifts request controls (Full Workspace)

  1. Choose the annual-leave request action in the section available to your account.
  2. Enter the first and last dates you want off, and review the estimated hours and balance shown.
  3. Check the dates carefully, especially if the request crosses your business’s leave-year boundary. Split it into the appropriate periods when required.
  4. Submit the request. Pending means a decision has not yet been recorded.
  5. Return to the request to check whether it is approved or declined. Speak to your Manager before treating requested dates as agreed holiday.
  6. Ask your Manager about changing an approved request. Do not assume removing a note or a calendar entry cancels an approval.
If the balance is unexpected

Ask your business to review your start date, contracted hours, working pattern, approved leave and configured leave year. You do not change your employer’s leave policy from the employee account.

Keep sickness separate

Full Workspace supports sickness reporting. Your business’s reporting procedure still applies.

  1. Notify your business through its agreed absence-reporting route.
  2. Use the sickness reporting action where it is available and enter the supported absence dates.
  3. Update your Manager about the expected return and tell them when you actually return.
  4. Do not enter diagnoses or unnecessary medical detail in operational notes. Sickness is separate from annual leave and does not use holiday balance.
