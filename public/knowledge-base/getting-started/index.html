Set up your business workspace

From your first sign-in to a checked pay schedule, a pilot team and your first published shift.

Business ownersClockx + Full Workspace
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role.

Your first setup checklist

Set the foundations before you invite the whole team. A short, controlled start makes the first working week easier.

Where to go Sign in > Business / Managers / Team

  1. Confirm your access. Use the Business Owner account supplied for your business. Check that the business name is yours and that you can see Business and Payroll. A standard Manager account has different access.
  2. Check the package. Confirm Clockx or Full Workspace, the employee allowance and any enabled add-ons. Ask DayRidge to correct the account configuration before building a workflow around a missing feature.
  3. Set the business basics. Review your business details, pay frequency, payday rule, weekly labour budget and automatic unpaid-break rule. Save, then check the calculated pay-period preview.
  4. Add a small pilot team. Check names, employee numbers, working patterns, start dates and access emails. Add your Managers separately from employee shift profiles.
  5. Run one complete shift. Publish a shift, confirm the employee can see it, record clock-in and clock-out, review breaks and check the resulting worked hours.
  6. Roll out to the rest of the team. Explain who handles questions, when rotas are published and how leave and corrections should be requested.
A useful first-day success measure

One employee can sign in, see a published shift, record attendance and find their hours. You can review that record from the owner workspace.

Choose the tools your business needs

Clockx provides the everyday staff-management essentials. Full Workspace adds wider people, document and cover workflows. Your business account determines the employee allowance and active tools.

Tasks and Stock Management are optional add-ons for Full Workspace. Ask DayRidge to activate the add-ons your business has agreed to use and confirm any applicable chargeable-user allowance.

Your agreement is the reference

Your agreed package, employee allowance and enabled add-ons determine the account you use. A hidden menu can reflect the package or role rather than a fault.

Set the business up correctly

These settings influence how the workspace identifies the business and how it presents budgets, pay periods and worked-time estimates.

Where to go Business > Business profile

  1. Review business details. Enter the business name, trading name, address, business email and phone number. Save a suitable business logo if you want one shown across supported areas.
  2. Choose pay frequency. Select Weekly, Fortnightly, Four-weekly or Monthly. For weekly pay, choose the payday weekday. For monthly pay, choose the day of the month.
  3. Set an anchor where required. Fortnightly and four-weekly cycles need an anchor payday so future paydays can repeat on the intended cycle.
  4. Check the preview. Review the next payday and pay-period dates before saving. Weekly cycles use the previous complete Monday-Sunday week; the monthly preview pays the previous calendar month.
  5. Set the weekly labour budget. Use a positive amount that reflects your planning budget. Budget estimates support rota planning; they are not the final payroll total.

If a chosen monthly payday does not exist in a particular month, the app uses that month’s last day. A business logo must be PNG, JPG or WebP and no larger than 2 MB.

Two settings to check separately

Automatic breaks are covered later in this guide. The annual leave year is managed by DayRidge administration: ask for the correct January-December or April-March policy to be configured.
