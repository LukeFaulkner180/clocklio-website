DAYRIDGE · TRAINING GUIDE

Set up Geo Clock-in for your workplace

Enable the add-on, choose the workplace on a map, set the radius, test employee arrival checks and handle Manager overrides.

Business owners · ManagersClockx or Full Workspace + Geo Clock-in add-on
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role.

Enable access and set the workplace

Geo Clock-in is optional for both packages. Existing businesses are not enrolled automatically.

Where to go Platform admin > Add-ons > Geo Clock-in; Business Owner > Business profile

  1. Agree your billing. Geo Clock-in is £4.99 per business per month or £49.99 per business per year. It covers everyone within your agreed employee allowance. Your core subscription is separate.
  2. Enable the business. Your DayRidge platform admin enables Geo Clock-in under Add-ons for the purchasing business. Enabling access does not collect payment.
  3. Open Business profile. The Business Owner finds the Geo Clock-in section. Other Managers cannot change the workplace location.
  4. Choose the workplace. Open Choose on map to place the pin. You can also use Set location from this device while at the premises, or enter accurate workplace latitude and longitude. Check the position before saving.
  5. Choose the radius. Enter a whole distance from 50 to 1,000 metres. Start with 100 metres and test it at the actual premises; device accuracy and building conditions vary.
  6. Save workplace location. Employees can then make personal-phone clock-ins that pass the location check. Until a location is saved, an enabled business cannot accept those clock-ins.
Use the workplace location

The device button fills the location of the device you are using, so use it at the premises. Map selection and manual coordinates can set the workplace without device-location permission. DayRidge does not look up the business address automatically.

Fine-tune the workplace on a map

Use a movable pin to choose the exact place employees should arrive. The map opens only when you choose it.

Where to go Business Owner > Business profile > Geo Clock-in > Choose on map

  1. Open the map. Choose on map displays your existing coordinates when available. With no saved point, navigate to the workplace using the map controls.
  2. Place or move the pin. Tap the map or drag the purple pin to the exact spot. You can also pan the map and choose Use map centre.
  3. Review the circle. The shaded area previews the allowed radius. Change Allowed radius (metres) to a whole value from 50 to 1,000, then choose Show allowed area to see the boundary.
  4. Check the coordinates and save. Moving the pin fills the latitude and longitude fields. Press Save workplace location to apply the changes. Moving a pin alone does not save.
Owner controls remain in place

Only the Business Owner can change and save the pin. Other Managers can view the saved workplace map. Selecting the workplace on a map does not remove the location check from employee personal-phone clock-ins.

If the map cannot load

Check the connection and try opening the map again. You can still use device location or enter accurate coordinates if this browser cannot display the map.

Test the area before staff use it

A short on-site check prevents avoidable clock-in problems on the first working day.

Where to go Employee View > My Time Clock

  1. Explain the check. Tell staff why a current location is requested and how to contact a Manager if it cannot be verified.
  2. Check inside the workplace. Ask a staff member with a real shift starting to test clock-in with precise location allowed. Do not create attendance records purely as a location test.
  3. Review the permitted area. An outside location is rejected. A poor-accuracy fix or one close to the boundary may also be rejected; move closer or retry with a clearer signal.
  4. Adjust if necessary. The Owner can refine the centre and radius in Business profile. Use an area that suits the actual premises.
Accuracy and limits

DayRidge checks a fresh device location and its reported accuracy. Device locations can be inaccurate or spoofed. Geo Clock-in is an arrival check, not a guarantee of physical presence.

Handle exceptions and permission problems

Keep a clear record when a legitimate arrival needs a Manager to intervene.

Where to go Business profile / Time clock > Team clock

  1. Location blocked. Check both browser and phone permissions. In Safari, allow Location for app.dayridge.co.uk. On iPhone, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Safari Websites, select While Using the App and turn Precise Location on. Refresh DayRidge afterwards. The setup screen includes Location permissions and help.
  2. Inaccurate or unavailable location. Check location services, try somewhere with a clearer signal and retry. An expired reading needs a new clock-in attempt.
  3. Record an authorised arrival. A Manager can use the existing team clock action with a reason of at least five characters. The reason stays with the attendance record and its audit history.
  4. Keep kiosk access available. Registered workplace kiosks retain their existing device/PIN flow. Clocking out does not require a location check.
  5. Change access through DayRidge. The platform admin can disable the add-on. Geo access remains available if a business changes between Full Workspace and Clockx.
Location privacy

There is no continuous employee tracking. The check happens when a personal-phone clock-in is requested. These Geo functions do not save raw employee coordinates.
