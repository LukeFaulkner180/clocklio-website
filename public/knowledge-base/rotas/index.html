Rotas: plan, publish and manage the week

Create and edit shifts, check clashes and leave, publish the rota, review budgets and use the month calendar.

Business owners · Managers · Team LeadersClockx + Full Workspace
Before you start

Your package and permissions control what appears. Team Leaders use only the sections their owner grants. Employee instructions refer to their own records; management controls need the appropriate role. Open shifts and cover offers require Full Workspace. Rota planning, personal schedules and the calendar are available in both packages.

Build the working week

The rota is a plan for who should work and when. It is separate from the record of hours actually worked.

Where to go Rota > Week planner

  1. Choose the week. Use the week arrows or This week. Check the dates displayed above the board before adding shifts.
  2. Add a shift. Use Add a shift or the plus control in a person’s day cell. Choose the employee, date, start and end times and role details shown in the form.
  3. Work in draft. Draft shifts let you prepare the plan before employees see it. Check coverage, repeated entries and the correct people.
  4. Review availability and leave. Approved leave appears on the board and blocks conflicting additions. Resolve a mismatch rather than forcing a shift onto an unavailable person.
  5. Check the labour picture. The owner can review planned hours and estimated costs against the weekly budget. Open shifts can contribute to planning totals where that tool is available.
  6. Publish when ready. Use the publishing control for the relevant week. Ask one employee to confirm the published shift appears in their personal view.
Draft is not published

A saved draft is still a planning record. It does not give employees a published shift. Check the published status after saving and after editing.

Publish, correct and find cover

Check the impact of a change before saving it. A clear process prevents the rota, employee expectations and actual attendance from drifting apart.

Where to go Rota / Open shifts / Activity

  1. 01Prepare draft
  2. 02Check conflicts
  3. 03Publish
  4. 04Review actual time
  • Editing a shift: open the shift chip and check the employee, date and times before saving. If you are changing an already communicated plan, tell the affected employee through your normal process.
  • Overlapping shifts: the app blocks clashes. Check whether the person already has another shift and whether the date or overnight end time is wrong.
  • Approved leave: do not work around a leave clash by creating a duplicate employee. Review the leave decision and intended dates first.
  • Removing a shift: read the confirmation and distinguish a planned rota shift from a completed worked-time entry. Removing a plan should not be treated as correcting a clocking record.
  • Sickness cover: supported Full Workspace rota controls can create an open cover offer for an eligible upcoming published shift. Review the absence and the dates first.
Check the result in both places

After a material change, review the manager rota and the employee’s published schedule. Use Activity to see recorded management changes and to help resolve questions.

Read the shift calendar

The month calendar brings planned work, recorded shifts and time away into a clear date-based view. Select a date for the underlying detail.

Where to go Employee > My shifts / Rota > Month calendar

Calendar colour key

ShiftsOvertimeSick / holiday
Split colours mean more than one type on the same date. Select the date to read the details.

  1. Choose whose calendar to view. In the Manager rota month view, select the team member. Employees see their own schedule.
  2. Move between months. Use the left and right arrows. Today returns you to the current date. The month changes directly; it is not a native month-selection wheel.
  3. Read the key. Green shows upcoming or completed shifts, orange overtime and purple time away such as approved holiday or recorded sickness. Split colours show more than one type on the same date.
  4. Select a day. Read the listed times and status rather than drawing conclusions from the colour alone. A past scheduled shift is not proof that the employee actually clocked in.
  5. Refresh when needed. Use the refresh control after a recent decision or correction. Check that the month and person still match the record you intended to review.

Common rota questions

Start with the person, dates and status before trying the same change again.

QuestionWhat to check
Why can an employee not see a shift?Check the employee record and week, then confirm that the shift is published. Drafts are for planning.
Why can I not add a shift?Check for approved leave, overlapping shifts and the employee selected in the form.
Does publishing record hours worked?No. Published shifts describe the plan; clockings and authorised worked-time entries record actual attendance.
Do I have to use the month picker?No. Left and right arrows change the calendar month directly. Select a date to read its details.
Can a Team Leader change the rota?Only if the owner grants Manage the team rota. Their supported controls are more focused than the Manager workspace.
Before you finish

Review the correct week, published status and employee view. Tell affected staff about material changes through your normal communication process.
