How the showcase works. Updated 22 October 2026.

Business tester registrations

The separate early access signup page sends and saves your name, email, business name, business type, team size, chosen interests, optional comments and contact permission. We use these details to organise business testing, provide early access and discuss the tester subscription offer. Please do not include employee records or sensitive information.

Registration details are stored privately, separately from customer workspaces. They are not displayed to other visitors. Registering does not create an app account, send an automatic confirmation email or start a paid subscription. Completing the agreed testing and feedback is required to qualify for the 10% annual subscription discount.

A short-lived, one-way network identifier is used to limit repeated signup attempts. The signup records do not include your raw IP address. Contact details are used for this programme, not added to an unrelated marketing list. You can decline an invitation or ask us to remove your registration by replying when we contact you. We keep registration details for the testing programme and follow-up, and remove them when they are no longer needed.

Contact form enquiries

When you submit the Contact form, DayRidge stores your name, email address, business name, selected package and team size, enquiry topic, optional referral reference and message. These details are used to review and respond to your enquiry.

Contact requests are available only to authorised DayRidge platform administrators in the management dashboard. The form does not send email notifications. Include only the information needed to discuss your business and the platform; do not submit employee records or sensitive information.

Website page views

We count public website page loads to understand how often the website is viewed. The counter keeps daily totals and does not use cookies, identify individual visitors, or store page URLs, referral query parameters or raw IP addresses. A random token for each page load prevents network retries from being counted twice; it is not saved in your browser. Tokens older than two days are cleared when new views are recorded. Repeat page loads count separately. The totals are visible only to authorised DayRidge platform administrators. Employee and manager app activity is separate.

Interactive examples

The product previews, feature filters, plan estimate and stock example run in your browser. Example staff, workspaces and figures are illustrative. The estimate is not a checkout or an agreed quotation.

Help tools and referral links

Help searches, update filters and the setup checklist run in your browser. The referral tool creates a shareable link containing the name or business reference you enter. This reference can be included in a Contact-page submission. Creating a link does not register a referral or create a reward.

Use a reference you are comfortable sharing in a URL. The website host receives page request addresses, which can include referral query parameters. The showcase does not submit referral records to a database. Avoid employee information and another person’s contact details.

External services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Following the sign-in link opens the separate DayRidge app. Those services handle requests under their own policies.

Employee and account information

This showcase does not display real employee records or customer workspaces. Employment records belong in your business’s signed-in workspace and are managed under the arrangements your employer provides.

Questions

You can submit a privacy question through the Contact page.
