DAYRIDGE LEGAL

Data Processing Agreement

How DayRidge processes business and employee records on behalf of its customers.
Version 2026-10-30 · Updated 30 October 2026.

Parties and scope

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is incorporated into the DayRidge Software Terms & Conditions and applies whenever the Customer, as controller, uses DayRidge, as processor, for personal data in its workforce workspace. It applies throughout paid use and free trials involving real personal data. The individual order identifies the Customer. It covers processing by DayRidge on behalf of the Customer, not data DayRidge controls independently for billing, accounts or sales.

Processing instructions

DayRidge processes Customer data only on the Customer’s documented instructions, including instructions expressed through authorised configuration and use of the service, unless UK law requires otherwise. We will inform the Customer of such a legal requirement unless prohibited. We will promptly notify the Customer if, in our opinion, an instruction breaches applicable data-protection law, without assuming responsibility for legal advice.

Processing details — Article 28 schedule

  • Subject matter: hosted workforce operations, rota planning, attendance and optional employee-management functions.
  • Duration: the trial or subscription term and the period needed for lawful data return/deletion afterwards.
  • Nature and purpose: collecting, storing, organising, displaying, transmitting, updating and deleting information to provide the selected service and customer-directed workflows.
  • Data subjects: Customer employees, applicants or workers, managers, business account holders and authorised contacts.
  • Data categories: names, emails, employee identifiers, working patterns, shifts, leave requests, attendance, pay-rate estimates, photos (where enabled), work-location checks, contracts, account and audit data; optional sickness/absence and onboarding records can include special-category or financial information.
  • Customer’s rights and obligations: set purposes and lawful bases, provide employee notices, decide record retention, configure permissions, issue lawful instructions and meet requests from data subjects.

Confidentiality and security

DayRidge will ensure personnel authorised to process Customer data are subject to confidentiality duties. We will implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk under Article 32, including access controls, role-based access, encrypted transport, account authentication, restricted administrative access and reasonable security testing and incident handling. Specific controls and any assurance materials can be discussed before onboarding. No particular certification or restoration time is promised by this DPA.

Sub-processors

The Customer gives general written authorisation for DayRidge to use sub-processors needed for hosted delivery, provided DayRidge places equivalent Article 28 obligations on each and remains responsible for their performance. Current infrastructure providers used by the service include Supabase (database/authentication) and Cloudflare (application delivery); transactional email and mapping providers may also be involved where those features are enabled. DayRidge will provide a current sub-processor list and available processing-location information on request, and give advance notice of intended additions or replacements, with a reasonable opportunity to object on data-protection grounds before a material change takes effect. Where necessary, suitable international-transfer safeguards will be used. No unsupported claim that all processing remains in the UK is made.

Data subject requests

Taking account of the processing, DayRidge will reasonably assist the Customer using appropriate technical and organisational measures with requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and other rights. DayRidge will pass on relevant requests it receives directly where appropriate, without independently deciding the Customer’s response except where law requires.

Incident handling and compliance assistance

DayRidge will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting its Customer data and provide reasonably available information and updates to help it assess the incident. DayRidge will reasonably assist with security obligations, breach notifications, impact assessments and prior consultation, taking account of information available to us and the nature of processing. The Customer remains responsible for deciding whether and how it reports to regulators and staff.

Audit and information

DayRidge will make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with its Article 28 obligations available to the Customer and allow for and contribute to audits and inspections by the Customer or an authorised independent auditor, subject to proportionate confidentiality, security and scheduling safeguards and without compromising other customers’ data.

Return and deletion on termination

At the end of processing services, the Customer may choose return or deletion of its personal data by contacting DayRidge. DayRidge will follow the documented choice and delete remaining copies unless a legal obligation requires retention. Where immediate removal from encrypted backups is technically impracticable, those copies will be isolated from ordinary use and deleted on the applicable secure retention cycle. Actual export formats, retention periods and deletion times should be agreed as part of the service exit plan before activation where material to the Customer.

Transfers, instructions and order of precedence

International transfers will be made only on documented instructions or lawful requirements and with an appropriate UK transfer mechanism where required. For Customer personal data, this DPA takes precedence over conflicting general Software Terms. The parties will cooperate in good faith if a regulatory change requires the DPA to be updated.

Contact

For questions about a trial DPA, subprocessors, employee records or a copy of the signed arrangement, Contact DayRidge. This document does not replace the Customer’s own staff privacy notice.
