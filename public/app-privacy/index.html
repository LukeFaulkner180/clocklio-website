DAYRIDGE LEGAL

Application Privacy Notice

The personal information DayRidge handles when you use the application.
Version 2026-10-30 · Updated 30 October 2026.

Who is responsible for your information

DayRidge is operated by Luke Faulkner trading as DayRidge. DayRidge acts as controller for its own account administration, security, contact, support and billing information. For employee workspace records uploaded by an employer, the employer is normally the controller and DayRidge is its processor. Employees should ask their employer first about their employment records and its privacy notice.

Information collected

Depending on your role and enabled features, the app may handle account email and identity, business contact details, login/security records, role permissions, support enquiries and subscription information. Employer-controlled workspaces may contain employee names, rosters, attendance, annual leave, sickness details, pay-rate estimates, workplace location checks, photographs, contracts and uploaded documents. Do not use optional features to collect more employee information than your business needs.

Why and on what basis

For DayRidge-controlled information, we use account and contact details to provide contracted services, answer enquiries, secure accounts, administer subscriptions and comply with legal obligations. Depending on the activity, the lawful basis is performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing a business service, or legal obligation. Your employer decides the lawful basis and any special-category condition for employment information it controls; DayRidge does not decide those employment purposes.

Who may receive data

Access is restricted by business membership, role and authorisation. Technical service providers supporting authentication, database hosting, application delivery, email and optional mapping may process data where required. DayRidge does not sell employee records. Your employer manages which authorised managers and staff can see its workspace records.

Retention and security

Account and business-administration records are kept for no longer than reasonably necessary for their purposes and legal obligations. Employer workspace information is retained and returned or deleted according to the Customer’s instructions and DPA, subject to technical and legal constraints. We use reasonable access controls and encrypted network connections; no internet service can guarantee zero security risk.

International transfers

Some providers may process limited information outside the UK. Where a restricted transfer is made, DayRidge will seek an appropriate UK GDPR transfer safeguard or lawful exception. Contact us for provider and transfer details relevant to a particular business.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase or restrict information, object, or request portability. For staff records, contact your employer as controller. For DayRidge's own controller data, Contact DayRidge. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Rights are subject to legal exceptions.

Website privacy and updates

The public marketing site's separate Website Privacy page explains forms and page-view analytics. We will update this notice when data uses change materially and publish the date/version above.
